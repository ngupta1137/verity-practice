Known Limitations & Engineering Roadmap

Transparency disclosure for Practice Owners and Clinical Directors evaluating Verity Practice. The following capabilities are explicitly deferred from this clinical prototype and scheduled for production milestones.

1. EHR Bi-Directional Integration DEFERRED

Direct API synchronization with outpatient nutrition EHRs (Healthie, Practice Better, Epic MyChart). Handout delivery currently writes to an encounter audit ledger rather than posting to patient charts.

2. Production Authentication & RBAC SIMULATED

Role-based access control (RD vs Front Desk vs Owner) is simulated via dropdown selector for interactive demonstration. Production requires OAuth 2.0 / SAML SSO with hardware MFA and session timeouts.

3. HIPAA Security Program & BAAs DEFERRED

This prototype utilizes strictly synthetic de-identified patient records. Operating on real patient protected health information requires executed Business Associate Agreements (BAAs), SOC 2 Type II audit, and encrypted backup lifecycle.

4. Payer-Aware MNT / DSMT Billing DEFERRED

Automated derivation of billable CPT units is explicitly prevented to prevent billing fraud. Commercial payer-specific pre-authorization rules, Medicare 3-hour initial limits, and DSMES accreditation cross-walks require licensed billing rules engine.

5. DSMES Accreditation Reporting DEFERRED

ADCES and ADA accreditation annual reporting formats (Standard 6 curriculum tracking, participant outcome monitoring, CQI audits) are deferred to dedicated compliance export modules.

6. Automated Guideline-Change Impact DEFERRED

Automated retrospective cohort identification across hundreds of patients when clinical consensus shifts. Currently demonstrated via the seeded Exposure Review Queue for manual clinician outreach.

7. Translation Governance & CLAS DEFERRED

Multilingual documents are managed via language concordance checks (Title VI / CLAS standards). Certified medical translation workflows and interpreter encounter attestations are planned for v2.

8. Downtime / Offline Delivery Procedure DEFERRED

Offline cached binder protocols and paper dispatch reconciliations during Internet or cloud provider service outages.

9. Proprietary Content Licensing DEFERRED

All 26 clinical education documents in this repository are synthetic prototypes authored for evaluation. Real practice deployments integrate licensed Academy Nutrition Care Manual (NCM) content or practice-owned proprietary IP.

10. Ephemeral Cloud Storage on Serverless (Vercel) SIMULATED

The live prototype is hosted on Vercel Serverless Functions where file-based storage (/tmp and local JSON files) is in-memory and ephemeral. State mutations (e.g. audit log entries, conflict resolution status) persist during an active container lifecycle but reset upon cold reboot or redeployment. A managed PostgreSQL / Supabase clinical data store is planned for production durability.
